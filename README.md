# COP31 Grid

Where I am during COP31, Antalya, 9 to 20 November 2026.

A single page: day, time, title, location, and whether a session is confirmed or part of the Global Renewables Hub programme. All times UTC+3.

Published at https://electrifysimon.github.io/cop31-grid/

## What this page deliberately leaves out

- Sessions that are not yet confirmed
- Any note on who else is attending, who I am supporting, or what a session needs from me
- Contact details, room-level access notes, hotels, dinners and bilateral meetings

If a session is not here, it is either not confirmed or not public.

## Adding a session

`docs/index.html` and `docs/data.json` are generated from a private register and published by hand. Nothing is automatic: a page that updates itself will one day announce a session a partner has not.

## Taking it down

Delete `docs/index.html` and push, or make the repository private. Either one removes the page within a minute.
